Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I book?

For peak season (June–August, December), we recommend 3–6 months in advance. For off-season travel, 6–8 weeks is usually sufficient. Custom itineraries need more lead time.

Do you offer custom itineraries?

Absolutely — it's our specialty. Every itinerary we build is crafted from scratch around your interests, budget, and pace. No templates, ever.

What is your cancellation policy?

Free cancellation up to 30 days before departure. 50% refund 15–29 days out. No refund within 14 days. We also offer flexible rebooking options.

Do you handle group travel?

Yes — family reunions, corporate retreats, friend groups, and destination weddings. We offer dedicated group coordinators and special rates for 8+ travelers.

Is travel insurance included?

Basic insurance is included in all our packages. We also offer premium comprehensive coverage as an add-on, which we strongly recommend for international trips.

How do payments work?

A 25% deposit secures your booking. The balance is due 45 days before departure. We accept all major cards, bank transfers, and offer interest-free installment plans.
